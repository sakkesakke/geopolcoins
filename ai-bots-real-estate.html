Questions

Is it free to try?

Yes. The Bot Garage is free and needs no account. The paid pack adds 10 finished bots with full prompts and test questions.

Do I need to code?

No. You copy a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or another AI tool. The Bot Garage can also export simple Telegram or Discord starter kits.

Will it make up prices or promises?

The bots are told never to invent prices, hours or availability, and to let you confirm bookings. AI can still make mistakes, so start by letting it draft replies you send yourself.

What about privacy?

The bots ask for as little personal data as possible and never for passwords or card numbers. Follow the privacy rules of your country, like GDPR in the EU.
