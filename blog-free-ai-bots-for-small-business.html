Free AI bots for salons, restaurants and real estate agents

6 November 2026 · 1 min read · Tool: Bot Garage

Also in: Nederlands Français Español Português Deutsch Türkçe Bahasa Indonesia

Quick answer

You do not need to pay for a simple AI assistant. Copy a ready-made bot prompt, fill in your business facts (hours, prices, address), paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and test it. Our Bot Garage has 24 free ones and needs no account.

What a small business bot can do

  • Answer the same ten questions every day: opening hours, prices, parking, payment.
  • Draft replies to reviews and messages in your tone.
  • Help with bookings and reminders (a human still confirms).
  • Write posts and offers for your social media.

The one thing that makes it useful

Bots fail when they do not know your business, so they guess. Give them a short "business card" with real facts and tell them to say "I don't know, I'll ask the team" when something is missing.

Three rules for every bot

  1. Never confirm a booking, price or discount on its own. The team confirms.
  2. No medical, legal or financial advice.
  3. Always offer a human when the customer asks or seems upset, and say clearly that it is an AI.
