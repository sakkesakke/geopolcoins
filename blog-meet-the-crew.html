Quick answer

Jean-Phil is the eccentric pixel genius, Bolt is the little robot guide who explains every tool, and Mevrouw Mintje is the pixel accountant who checks tokens for red flags. Together they run the free arcade on geopolcoins.xyz.

Jean-Phil

The brain of the crew, with a moustache and a wardrobe that screams genius degen. He hosts the videos, stars as the ice knight in MAIGA Raid, and runs the garage where you build AI bots.

Bolt

A small yellow robot and the guide on every page. Ask Bolt anything about the site and he explains the tool in 10 languages. He is a simple help guide, not an AI chatbot, so nothing you type leaves your device. In the Bot Garage he is the mechanic.

Mevrouw Mintje

The pixel accountant with the glasses. She reads public token data in the Token Checkup and on the MAIGA Radar, and says plainly what looks fine, what looks risky and what she cannot see.

Lord Rugpull

Every arcade needs a villain. Lord Rugpull waits at the top of the Rug Tower in MAIGA Raid. Beat him for a spot on the weekly leaderboard. No wallet, no prizes, just bragging rights.
