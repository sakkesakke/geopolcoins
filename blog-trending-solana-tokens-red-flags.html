Trending Solana tokens: what the red flags actually mean

6 November 2026 · 1 min read · Tool: MAIGA Radar

Also in: Nederlands Français Español Português Deutsch

Quick answer

"Trending" only means many people trade a token right now. It says nothing about whether it will go up or whether it is safe. Read the flags first: thin liquidity, a pool under one day old, no sells, volume far above liquidity, or a huge 24-hour spike.

Why trending lists attract scams

Getting onto a trending list brings free attention, so some projects fake activity with bots. A busy-looking token can be a few wallets trading with each other.

The flags on our Radar

  • Thin liquidity (under $10,000): small sales move the price a lot and you may not get out.
  • Under 1 day old: no track record. Most rugs happen early.
  • No sells: many buys and zero sells can mean selling is blocked.
  • Volume far above liquidity: possible fake volume from bots.
  • Pumped or crashed: +300% or -60% in 24 hours. Late buyers usually pay.

What a risk label means

"High risk" means several serious flags. "Fewer flags" does not mean safe; it means it passed these few checks. For mint and freeze authority, holders and the creator wallet, open the full check.
