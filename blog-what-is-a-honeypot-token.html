What is a honeypot token, and how do you spot one?

6 November 2026 · 1 min read · Tool: Token Checkup

Also in: Nederlands Français Español Português Deutsch Türkçe Bahasa Indonesia

Quick answer

A honeypot token is built so that buying works but selling fails or is taxed to almost nothing. The clearest public sign is many buys and no sells. Check before you buy; afterwards it is too late.

How a honeypot works

The trick lives in the token's rules or the pool setup. The price chart only goes up, because nobody can sell. That rising chart is the bait. People buy, try to sell, and discover they cannot.

Warning signs you can see

  • Buys but no sells in the last 24 hours, for example 58 buys and 0 sells.
  • A flag from a data source: GeckoTerminal marks some tokens as a possible honeypot.
  • Freeze authority still on: the creator can freeze your account so you cannot move tokens.
  • Price only goes up in a perfect line, often on a brand-new pool with little liquidity.

What you can do

  1. Paste the address into the free Token Checkup and read every flag.
  2. Look at the recent trades on an explorer: do you see real sells by different wallets?
  3. If you still want to try, never use money you cannot lose.

No public check catches every honeypot. Education only, not financial advice.

Questions

Can I get my money back from a honeypot?

Usually not. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Prevention is the only real protection.

Does 'no honeypot flag' mean it is safe?

No. It only means the data source saw no sign that selling is blocked.
