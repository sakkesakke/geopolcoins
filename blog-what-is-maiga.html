Quick answer

$MAIGA ("Make AI Great Again") is a satirical meme coin on Solana. It is a joke and a community mascot for a set of free tools and games on geopolcoins.xyz. It is not an investment product, it has no roadmap promises, and it can lose all its value.

What it is

  • A token on Solana, launched on pump.fun, that moved to a normal trading pool in October 2026.
  • The mascot of a free website: games, learning labs, a meme maker and AI bot tools.
  • Satire about AI hype and crypto hype. The site itself teaches people to spot scams.

What it is not

  • Not a company share, not a stablecoin, not a promise of returns.
  • Not needed for anything on the site. Every tool is free without a wallet or a token.
  • Not financial advice. We never predict its price.

The risks, plainly

Meme coins are extremely risky. At the time of writing $MAIGA has little liquidity and a small number of holders, and a large share sits in a few wallets.
