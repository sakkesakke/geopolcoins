Cookie and storage policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. The English text is the reference version. Nederlands: vragen in het Nederlands zijn welkom. Français : questions en français bienvenues.

In short: geopolcoins.xyz does not set cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. That is why there is no cookie banner. Some tools save settings in your browser's local storage, only to do what you asked for.

1. Cookies

Our own pages set no cookies. Our visit counter works without cookies and without storing any identifier on your device.

2. Local storage on your device

Local storage is a small storage area in your browser. Our pages use it only for functions you use. The data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Under the EU ePrivacy rules this storage is allowed without consent because it is strictly necessary for a service you request.

3. Third parties

The swap widget on the home page is loaded from Jupiter and may use its own storage under Jupiter's policy. When you click a link to another site (Ko-fi, Discord, X, Pump.fun, Solscan, DexScreener and others), that site's own cookies and policies apply. Images from memegen.link and data from GeckoTerminal are loaded without cookies from our side.

4. How to delete it

You can delete all stored data at any time in your browser settings (look for "site data", "cookies and site data" or "clear browsing data" for geopolcoins.xyz). If you installed the app, uninstalling it also removes its storage. Deleting it removes your saved bots and settings.
