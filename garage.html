Where can my bot live? (and the rules there)
  • ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini: paste the prompt as the instructions of a custom assistant. Easiest, no code.
  • Telegram: make a bot with @BotFather, put the token in the kit's .env, run one command. It only answers people who message it.
  • Discord: create an app in the Discord Developer Portal, enable the Message Content intent, invite it to your server. It answers when it is mentioned or in DMs.
  • X: the kit's X poster only posts from a queue you approve, once per run, on your own account. X's automation rules ban automated likes, follows, trend posting and unsolicited or keyword-based replies, and AI reply bots need X's written approval. Mark the account as automated in X's settings.
