Exam button: no AI during the test

One tap switches every AI chatbot off in the browsers of your class, for as long as the test lasts. Free, no accounts, no pupil names.

1. Make your class

You get a class code for the pupils and a private teacher link for you.

Your class code

  1. Pupils install Human Override in their browser (school computers: once).
  2. They open the extension, go to “Class · exam mode”, type this code and tap Join.
  3. The school or teacher sets the PIN in the extension (not the pupil), so a pupil cannot leave the class.

Keep the teacher link private: who has it can start and stop the exam. Save it as a bookmark to use the class on another device.

2. Exam mode

Honest limits: it works in browsers where the extension joined your class, and it can take up to one minute before every browser follows. It does not block phones or other browsers, and a pupil who controls the whole computer can remove the extension: on school computers, install it with browser policy. All AI categories are blocked during the exam, also AI your school normally approves.
