What it does

✋ Blocks 130+ AI services

Chatbots, AI search, image, video, voice, music, writing, homework and coding tools. The list updates itself daily.

🔒 PIN lock

With a PIN set, nobody can switch it off or change the list without the code.

⚙️ Your choice

Pick categories, add your own sites, or always allow one you trust.

🕵️ Private

No account, no tracking, no ads. It never reads your pages; it only downloads the public block list.

Install in 1 minute

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it (right click, "Extract all").
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin the red ✋ icon to your toolbar, open it and set a PIN.

Honest limits

Human Override blocks AI services in this browser. It cannot block AI built into apps you allow (for example AI answers inside a search engine), AI that runs fully offline on the device, or other browsers and apps. Someone with access to the computer could remove the extension; schools and companies can force-install it with browser policies. A phone and whole-network version is in the works.
