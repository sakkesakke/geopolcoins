Install in 1 minute

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it (right click, "Extract all").
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin the red ✋ icon to your toolbar, open it and set a PIN.
  6. Important for parents and schools: on the extension's "Details" page, switch on "Allow in Incognito", otherwise private windows are not blocked.

Honest limits

Human Override blocks AI services in this browser. It cannot block AI built into apps you allow (for example AI answers inside a search engine), AI that runs fully offline on the device, or other browsers and apps. Someone with access to the computer could remove the extension; schools and companies can force-install it with browser policies. For phones and whole networks, see our DNS lists.
