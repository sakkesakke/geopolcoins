Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The browser extension, the Android app, the Windows, Mac and Linux versions and the Pro pack are free. You can support the work with a donation; organisations can ask for paid help with roll-out.

Does it collect my data?

No. There is no account and no tracking. Blocking happens on your own device. The only download is the public block list.

Can my child or employee just turn it off?

Not in the app or extension without the PIN. Someone with full control of the device can still uninstall it, so use a normal (non-admin) account, Google Family Link on Android, or browser policies at school and work.

Does it block AI inside Google, WhatsApp or Instagram?

It blocks Google’s AI Mode and dedicated AI sites and apps. AI built into apps you keep using (like an assistant inside a messenger) cannot always be removed without blocking the whole app.
