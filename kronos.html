How Kronos makes a forecast

  1. Candles become wordsEach hour has an open, high, low, close and volume. Kronos turns every candle into tokens, the way a chatbot turns text into tokens.
  2. It learned the patternsTrained on years of charts from many markets, it learned which shapes often come after which, like a language model learns which word comes next.
  3. It writes possible futuresGiven 360 hours of history, it writes the next 24 hours, 6 times. Each try is a bit different, because markets are random.
  4. The spread is the honestyA wide band means "I really don't know". The middle line is only the average of its guesses, not a promise.

Myths, answered straight

"AI can predict crypto prices."

Not reliably. Look at the scoreboard: even a serious research model is often wrong, and sometimes "the price stays the same" is a better guess. Anyone selling a sure AI prediction is selling a story.

"If it worked on the past, it works tomorrow."

No. Markets change, and a few dozen moments can look good by luck. Trading costs and fees also eat small edges. That is why this lab only shows the past and never gives live forecasts.

"Is this a signal for $MAIGA?"

No. Kronos is never run on $MAIGA, and nothing here says what any coin will do. $MAIGA is a satirical meme coin. Entertainment only. NFA. DYOR.

"Where does the data come from?"

Public hourly price data (no account, no keys) and the open-source Kronos-small model. A script runs every week, forecasts past moments only, and saves the results here. Nothing is traded.
