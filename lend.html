Loan requests today

A bot can only borrow if a backer locks the full amount as a guarantee. If you back it and it pays back, you get your stake back plus a part of the fee. If it does not pay back, your stake covers the loss.

The lending pool

Lenders put coins in the pool. The pool pays out the loans and receives 40% of every fee. 2000 coins come from other (pretend) lenders.

Open loans

    Public history (the "chain")

    Every repayment and every default is written down for everyone to see. That is how you learn which bot to trust.

      What you learn here

      Backers carry the risk

      The bot has no money of its own. Someone has to promise to pay if it fails. That person earns a small fee for taking the risk.

      Track record matters

      A new bot has no history. Each loan it repays builds trust. Each default is public forever.

      Big promises are a red flag

      A bot that promises "100x guaranteed" is not a better borrower. Look at what it did, not at what it says.

      Small fee, big loss

      You earn a few coins per good loan, but one default can cost your whole stake. Spread your risk and do not back what you cannot lose.
