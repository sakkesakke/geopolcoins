We never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or passwords.

3. What stays on your device

Many tools work only in your browser and keep their settings in your browser's local storage on your own device. We cannot see this data. Examples: language choice, your saved bots, recent token checks, game best scores, and, only if you choose "remember", your own AI key. See the Cookie and storage policy for the full list and how to delete it.

Your AI key (Meme Factory, Bot Garage): it is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you pick (Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter), never to us. Your idea or prompt then goes to that provider under its own privacy policy, and that provider bills you.

Meme Studio: your photo and your meme are made on your device and are not uploaded to us.

4. Services that receive data from your browser

Some features load data from, or send you to, independent services. When that happens, your browser connects to them directly and they can see your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies:

  • GeckoTerminal (coin info, trending list, Token Checkup): we request public token data.
  • memegen.link (classic meme templates in the Meme Studio and Factory).
  • Jupiter (swap widget on the home page): its script loads on the home page; swaps are signed in your own wallet.
  • Phantom or your wallet: only when you choose to connect or sign.
  • Ko-fi: tips and digital products are sold and paid through Ko-fi, which handles payment data as its own controller. We receive the order details Ko-fi gives sellers (such as name, email and what you bought) to deliver and support your purchase.
  • Discord, X, Pump.fun, Solscan, DexScreener and other links: only when you click them.

8. Your rights (worldwide)

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy (portability);
  • correct it;
  • delete it ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to its use, including any use based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for example remove your public post), without affecting earlier use.

Email us at geopolcoins1@gmail.com and tell us your pilot name, nickname or email so we can find your data. We may ask for proof that the data is yours (for example a signed message from the same wallet). We answer within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, as the GDPR allows) and free of charge.

If you think we got it wrong, you can complain to a data protection authority: the authority in the country where you live or work (for example the CNDP in Morocco, any EU data protection authority, the ICO in the UK, the ANPD in Brazil, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner in Canada or the OAIC in Australia). We would appreciate the chance to fix it first.

These rights follow Moroccan Law 09-08, the EU and UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Switzerland's FADP, Australia's Privacy Act, Japan's APPI, Singapore's PDPA, South Korea's PIPA, India's DPDP Act and similar laws. We give the same rights to everyone, wherever you live.

10. Children

This site is not meant for children. You must be at least 16 to use features that store data (or the minimum age for consent in your country, if higher), and 18 or the age of majority to take part in tournaments or prizes. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (US COPPA) or under 16. If you believe a child has sent us data, email us and we will delete it.

11. Blockchain data

Prize payouts and swaps happen on the public Solana blockchain. Wallet addresses and transactions there are public and permanent, and nobody (including us) can change or delete them. Apart from payout records, which we publish as proof of payment, we do not show wallet addresses on our site. Anyone can look up a transaction on the blockchain.

12. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), strict database access rules (row level security), rate limits, automatic secret scanning and code scanning. Only the admin account can read custom bot requests and the wallet addresses entered for prizes or verification. Payout records are public by design (see section 11). No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights, we will tell the authority and, where required, you, within the legal deadlines.

13. Changes

We update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be mentioned on the home page.
