    Questions

    Do I need to code?

    No. You click blocks together in tools like Make or a voice agent platform and paste our prompts. You do need to be comfortable creating accounts and copying API keys. If that sounds like too much, ask us to set it up.

    Will the bot make up prices or promise things?

    Every prompt forbids it: the bot only uses the facts on your business card and says "I'll check with the team" when something is missing. AI can still make mistakes, which is why every blueprint has test questions and starts small.

    Is it allowed to answer calls with an AI?

    Usually yes, if callers know they are talking to an AI. Recording calls and sending texts have extra rules that differ per country. The blueprints point out where to check; this is not legal advice.

    What about privacy (GDPR)?

    Collect only what the bot needs, tell customers what you collect, delete what you no longer need and pick providers that offer a data processing agreement.

    Which languages work?

    The prompts are in English and tell the bot to answer in the customer's language. Most voice platforms support French, Dutch, Spanish, Arabic and more; check the voice you pick.

    Is there a free version?

    Yes: the Bot Garage has 24 free chat bots you can copy into ChatGPT or Claude, and the AI bot Q&A answers the common questions.
