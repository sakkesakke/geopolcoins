Questions

What do the red flags mean?

They are automatic checks on public pool data: very thin liquidity, a pool younger than a day, buys without any sells, trading volume far above the liquidity, and huge 24-hour price jumps or crashes. A token without flags is not safe; it only passed these few checks. Open the full Token Checkup for mint and freeze authority, holders and the creator wallet.

Is this a trading signal?

No. Trending means many people trade it right now, not that it will go up. Most new tokens lose almost all their value. The radar shows public facts and risks only. Not financial advice.

Where does the data come from?

Your browser loads it directly from public free sources: GeckoTerminal (Solana pools), CoinGecko (top coins), alternative.me (Fear and Greed index) and DefiLlama (DeFi value locked). No account and no wallet are needed. Data can be late or wrong.
