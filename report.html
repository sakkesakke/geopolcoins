Questions

What is the Solana Red Flag Report?

A free weekly count of public red flags across the trending Solana pools on GeckoTerminal. We sample the trending list four times a day and count each pool once per week.

Which red flags do you count?

Thin liquidity under $10,000, many buys with zero sells (a honeypot sign), 24-hour volume over 50 times the liquidity (possible bot volume), and price moves of +300% or -60% in 24 hours. A serious red flag means thin liquidity or buys without sells.

Does a pool without flags mean it is safe?

No. These are a few public checks only. Many risks do not show in public data. This is education, not financial advice.
