Safety comes first, every time

Robots can hurt people and break things. This is how we keep the AI in its place.

  • The AI never drives the motorsIt only proposes commands through the robot's normal interface. The robot's certified safety system keeps the final say.
  • A human approvesEvery plan that moves something waits for a clear yes. Emergency stops always work and always win.
  • Hard rules, not opinionsNo-go zones, speed and payload limits are checked by fixed rules. Unsafe orders are refused, not "negotiated".
  • Everything is loggedEach order, plan, approval and stop is written down, so you can see who decided what.
  • Start in simulationNew tasks are tested in a simulation or with the robot in a safe mode first.
  • Rules for machines and AIWe work with your safety officer and supplier. In the EU, the Machinery Regulation and the AI Act apply to safety-related AI.

Questions

Which robots does this work with?

Any robot or fleet that has a documented way to send commands or read status, for example through its vendor software, an API or ROS 2. For the help bot, manuals and service notes are enough.

Do we need to change our robots?

No hardware changes. The AI layer runs next to your existing software, and the robot's own safety settings stay as they are.

Where does our data go?

We agree on that before we start. Manuals and logs can stay on your own servers, and we tell you exactly which AI service is used and what it receives.

Is this connected to the $MAIGA coin?

No. This is a normal service paid by invoice. No token, no crypto, nothing on-chain.
