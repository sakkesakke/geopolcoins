Questions

What is a rug pull?

A crypto scam where the people behind a token take the money out, for example by pulling the liquidity, printing new tokens or blocking sales. The price crashes to almost zero and buyers cannot get out.

Which red flags point to a rug pull?

Mint authority still on, freeze authority still on, liquidity that is not locked or burned, very thin liquidity, one creator or a few wallets holding most of the supply, and many buys with zero sells (a honeypot). No flags does not mean safe.
